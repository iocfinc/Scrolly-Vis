# Story Arc

## Default arc

1. Baseline
2. Change or comparison
3. Tension or anomaly
4. Interpretation
5. Implication

## Step design checks

- Does each step introduce one new idea?
- Does the visual state change enough to justify the scroll?
- Can the reader understand the key point in under five seconds?
- Is the final step a conclusion rather than a repeated highlight?

## Good scrollytelling patterns

- Reveal one layer of complexity at a time.
- Keep a stable visual frame when possible so change is perceptible.
- Use annotation sparingly and only where the data needs help.
- Repeat visual encodings consistently across steps.
