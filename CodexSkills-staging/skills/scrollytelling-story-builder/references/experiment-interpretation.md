## Narrative prompts

- What was the expected result?
- What result actually happened?
- Which subgroup, segment, or time period matters most?
- Where is the strongest evidence?
- What caveat would a careful reader ask about?

## Dataset-to-story checklist

- Define units clearly.
- Name the time window.
- Separate descriptive findings from causal claims.
- Flag missing data or methodological limits.
- End with the implication, not just the number.
